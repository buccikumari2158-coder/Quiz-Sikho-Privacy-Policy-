Privacy Policy for Quiz Sikho

Application: Quiz Sikho (com.quizsikho.myapp)
Effective Date: November 25, 2026
Developer Contact: barshanayaknayak916@gmail.com

Quiz Sikho is an educational bilingual quiz application designed for children (Nursery through Class 5, ages 3–10) and families. Protecting children's privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains clearly what information the app uses, how it is stored, and how third-party services operate inside the app.

Summary for Parents: Quiz Sikho does not require email registration, does not upload your child's name, class, age, or quiz progress to cloud servers, and strictly serves child-safe, non-personalized G-rated advertisements with Advertising ID collection disabled.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

During onboarding and gameplay, the app allows the user to enter a display name/nickname, select a school class (Nursery, LKG, UKG, or Class 1st–5th), choose an age bracket (3–5, 5–7, or 7–10), and pick from 10 built-in illustrated avatars. This profile information, along with earned coins, diamonds, 5-level streaks, completed/wrong level history, customizable question timer (5s, 10s, 20s, 30s, or 60s), sound effects toggle, and language choice (English, Hindi, or Bilingual Default), is stored strictly locally on the device using Android Jetpack DataStore and SharedPreferences.

  • We do not upload or transmit your child's name, class, age, avatar, or quiz scores to any external server or database.
  • Android cloud backup (allowBackup="false") is disabled so personal profile preferences are not synced externally.

2. Firebase Anonymous Authentication, Cloud Quiz Content & Offline Cache

Quiz Sikho uses Google Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore solely to fetch updated educational quiz questions and categories (quiz_levels) published by the app administrator.

  • Anonymous Authentication generates a random, temporary technical session identifier without collecting any name, email address, phone number, or personal details.
  • No child profile information or gameplay progress is written to Firestore.
  • Once quiz questions are synced online, the Firestore SDK keeps a local offline cache on the device so previously loaded quiz levels remain playable when the device is temporarily offline.
  • Google Firebase Analytics collection and Advertising ID analytics collection are explicitly deactivated in the app configuration.

3. Advertising & Children's Privacy (COPPA / Google Play Families Policy)

Quiz Sikho displays banner and occasional level-transition interstitial ads using Google AdMob in strict compliance with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA:

  • Child-Directed Treatment: The AdMob SDK is initialized with TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE and TAG_FOR_UNDER_AGE_OF_CONSENT_TRUE.
  • G-Rated Content Only: Maximum ad content rating is restricted to G (General Audiences).
  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: Every ad request explicitly passes npa=1 (non-personalized ads), disabling behavioral targeting and interest-based profiling.
  • No Advertising ID Collection: The Android AD_ID permission and Privacy Sandbox AdServices tracking permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) are removed from the app manifest.

For more information on how Google handles data in child-directed AdMob requests, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Educational Question Images

Some quiz questions load educational illustrations over HTTPS (including automatic upgrade of image URLs to HTTPS) from static image hosting links (such as ImgBB i.ibb.co). Image requests fetch only the picture resource itself and do not send personal user identifiers or tracking data.

5. Device Permissions

Quiz Sikho declares only minimal install-time permissions needed for network quiz loading and child-safe ads:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (for syncing quiz questions and displaying non-personalized family-safe ads)
  • Standard Google Play Services technical permissions (WAKE_LOCK, READ_GSERVICES) merged by the Firebase/AdMob SDKs
  • AD_ID, Privacy Sandbox AdServices permissions, and FOREGROUND_SERVICE are explicitly removed from the app manifest.

The app requests zero dangerous runtime permissions and does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone state, or external photo/file storage.

6. Parental Controls & Data Deletion

Because all child profile details and progress are stored locally on the device and never collected on our servers:

  • Parents or guardians can edit the child's display name, class, age group, avatar, language, sound, or timer settings anytime inside the app's Settings screen.
  • To permanently delete all local profile data, coins, diamonds, and level history, simply clear the app's storage in Android device settings or uninstall Quiz Sikho, or contact us at barshanayaknayak916@gmail.com for assistance.
